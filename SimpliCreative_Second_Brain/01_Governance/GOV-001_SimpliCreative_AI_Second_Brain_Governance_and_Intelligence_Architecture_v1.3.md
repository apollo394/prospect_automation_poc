## Purpose

This document defines the governing architecture for the SimpliCreative
AI Second Brain. It establishes how the system should:

  ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Area**    **Requirement**
  ----------- ------------------------------------------------------------
  Knowledge   Organize and retrieve approved agency intelligence

  Authority   Determine which sources may govern a task

  Context     Assemble the information required for meaningful reasoning

  Evidence    Distinguish evidence from inference and uncertainty

  Memory      Separate working, episodic, semantic, and reference
              knowledge

  Client Data Maintain strict client isolation

  Decisions   Preserve rationale, approvals, implementation, and outcomes

  Learning    Promote observations into durable knowledge only through
              controlled review

  External    Use methodologies and reference materials without allowing
  Sources     them to become agency strategy

  AI Output   Apply Brand Voice, AI Pattern Detection, evidence, and
              originality controls

  Human       Preserve human accountability for consequential decisions
  Judgment    
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------

Individual strategies, SOPs, and engineering specifications define how
specific work is performed. This document defines how the Intelligence
Layer interprets and uses those sources together.

## 1. Engineering North Star

The Second Brain should make SimpliCreative's collective intelligence
available when the team needs it so people can make better decisions
without repeatedly reconstructing context. The system should understand
more than what a document says. It should understand:

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Question**         **Required Understanding**
  -------------------- --------------------------------------------------
  What is this         Strategy, evidence, methodology, example, client
  information?         fact, decision, research, etc.

  Is it current?       Current, historical, or superseded

  What may it govern?  Authority domain

  Who does it belong   Agency, client, prospect, external source
  to?                  

  How reliable is it?  Evidence and validation state

  What restrictions    Client, source, reuse, confidentiality
  apply?               

  Has a decision       Decision and approval state
  already been made?   

  Is human judgment    Approval or escalation requirement
  still required?      
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

The central operating rule is:

    RELEVANCE DETERMINES WHAT TO RETRIEVE
    AUTHORITY DETERMINES WHAT MAY GOVERN

## 2. System Goals

The Second Brain should: Reduce knowledge loss. Improve consistency.
Accelerate research and preparation. Preserve strategic reasoning.
Strengthen handoffs. Make approved agency knowledge Findable™. Protect
client-specific knowledge. Improve access to evidence and decision
history. Support higher-quality strategic and operational decisions.
Create compounding organizational intelligence. Prevent AI from silently
redefining SimpliCreative strategy. Success should be evaluated through
accuracy, grounding, source authority, strategic alignment,
client-context fidelity, evidence discipline, prioritization, voice,
restraint, actionability, traceability, client isolation, and human
adoption. The system should preserve uncertainty when the evidence is
incomplete.

## 5. Evidence & Diagnostic Reasoning

The Second Brain must distinguish the symptom being reported from the
conditions that may be contributing to it. Every material finding should
preserve an appropriate evidence state.

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Evidence        **Meaning**
  State**           
  ----------------- -----------------------------------------------------
  Observed          Directly visible in available evidence

  Reported          Stated by prospect, client, stakeholder, or approved
                    source

  Inferred          Reasonable interpretation of available evidence

  Hypothesized      Potential explanation requiring validation

  Directional       Evidence suggests a pattern but does not establish it

  Validated         Supported by sufficient relevant evidence

  Partially         Some evidence supports the conclusion
  Validated         

  Contradicted      Material evidence conflicts with the conclusion

  Unsupported       Available evidence does not support the claim

  Unknown           Required evidence is unavailable
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

AI confidence does not improve evidence quality. Use:

    AI_CONFIDENCE != EVIDENCE
    SPECIFICITY_DOES_NOT_AUTHORIZE_DIAGNOSIS
    SUMMARIZATION_MUST_NOT_INCREASE_CERTAINTY
    CORRELATION != CAUSATION

## 9. Inbound Acquisition

SimpliCreative's acquisition system is intentionally inbound. Owned
content, paid media, search, PR, partner distribution, website
experiences, and approved audience-development inputs should help
qualified buyers recognize relevant marketing friction and progress
toward the Lean Marketing Assessment. The governing acquisition sequence
is:

    CREATE RECOGNITION → REFRAME THE PROBLEM → INVITE DIAGNOSIS

The Assessment identifies where friction appears. It does not establish
root cause.

    SCORECARD_RESULT != ROOT_CAUSE

Acquisition should ultimately be evaluated by buyer quality rather than
lead volume. Coldlytics may be used as audience seed data for
appropriate inbound paid-media audience development.

    COLDLYTICS = AUDIENCE_SEED_DATA_NOT_OUTBOUND_LEAD_LIST

Campaign learning does not automatically become agency strategy.

    CAMPAIGN_PATTERN != AGENCY_TRUTH
    PROSPECT_PATTERN != AGENCY_RULE

## 11. Paid-Media Methodology Authority

SimpliCreative strategy determines who the agency serves, what it
believes, what it offers, the problem it addresses, its point of view,
and how the brand communicates. Approved paid-media methodology may
govern execution within its authorized domain. Ashley's Paid Ads
Playbook is therefore:

    SUPPORTING_METHODOLOGY

It is not SimpliCreative agency strategy. The authority hierarchy is:

    SIMPLICREATIVE STRATEGY

↓

    SIMPLICREATIVE MESSAGING + BRAND VOICE

↓

    BUYER STAGE + MESSAGE TERRITORY + OFFER

↓

    ASHLEY PAID-MEDIA METHODOLOGY

↓

    PLATFORM-SPECIFIC EXECUTION

Use:

    ASHLEY_METHODOLOGY_MAY_OPTIMIZE_MESSAGE_EXECUTION_BUT_MAY_NOT_OVERRIDE_APPROVED_SIMPLICREATIVE_STRATEGY

Within Ashley's methodology, current coaching protocols override
lower-level curriculum when those sources conflict. Scenario files are
reference examples only. They may illustrate a methodology. They do not
independently create SimpliCreative campaign policy.

    PAIN_FIRST = RECOGNIZABLE_FRICTION_FIRST_NOT_FEAR_FIRST

Specificity does not authorize diagnosis.

    SPECIFICITY_DOES_NOT_AUTHORIZE_DIAGNOSIS

    CONVERSION_OPTIMIZATION_MUST_NOT_CREATE_UNSUPPORTED_URGENCY_OR_CLAIMS

## 14. Brand Voice

The Second Brain should communicate as a trusted strategic advisor. The
desired voice is calm, executive, insightful, practical, collaborative,
specific, and confident without overstating certainty. The preferred
editorial movement is:

    OBSERVATION → INSIGHT → EXPLANATION → RECOMMENDATION → INVITATION

This is a reasoning pattern. It is not a mandatory paragraph or copy
formula.

## 15. AI Pattern Detection

Substantive AI-generated external content must pass AI Pattern Detection
before delivery. This includes: Website copy. Thought leadership. Paid
advertising. Landing pages. Nurture. Partner content. Sales materials.
Proposals. Blueprint deliverables. Presentations. CARE reporting
narrative. Other substantive external communication. Pattern Detection
should evaluate repetitive three-sentence structures, surface-level
language, unnecessary em-dash dependence, generic AI filler, repetitive
contrast framing, predictable rhetorical structures, excessive
templating, and other patterns defined in the current Brand Voice &
Editorial Standards. The goal is not to prohibit individual writing
techniques. The goal is to prevent recognizable machine-generated
patterns from becoming SimpliCreative's default voice. Use:

    EXTERNAL_AI_GENERATED_CONTENT_REQUIRES_AI_PATTERN_DETECTION

Example copy must meet the same standard.

    ALL_EXAMPLE_COPY_MUST_PASS_AI_PATTERN_DETECTION_BEFORE_BEING_USED_AS_REFERENCE
    EXAMPLE_COPY_DEMONSTRATES_PRINCIPLE_NOT_PREFERRED_WORDING
    REFERENCE_EXAMPLE_MUST_NOT_BECOME_REUSABLE_COPY_TEMPLATE

## 16. Originality & Source Integrity

Reference material may inform analysis, reasoning, structure, research
direction, content needs, and quality. It does not authorize copying.
This applies to external frameworks, competitor websites, themes and
demos, Gold-Standard examples, client deliverables, expert resources,
historical work, and other reference sources. Use:

    REFERENCE_MATERIAL_INFORMS_WORK_IT_DOES_NOT_AUTHORIZE_COPYING
    EXPERT_FRAMEWORK_DOES_NOT_AUTHORIZE_COPYING
    REFERENCE_EXAMPLE_DOES_NOT_AUTHORIZE_REUSE
    CLIENT_DELIVERABLE_DOES_NOT_BECOME_COPY_BANK
    CLIENT_SPECIFIC_CONTENT_MUST_REMAIN_CLIENT_BOUND
    SOURCE_RESTRICTIONS_MUST_SURVIVE_SYNTHESIS

AI may flag potential material source similarity. AI must not certify
legal originality, copyright safety, plagiarism status, or fair use.

    POTENTIAL_SOURCE_SIMILARITY_REQUIRES_HUMAN_REVIEW
    AI_MUST_NOT_CERTIFY_LEGAL_ORIGINALITY

## 17. Website & Digital Foundation

The website is one of the few digital environments the organization
controls. It can provide a consistent environment for positioning,
messaging, credibility, expertise, buyer education, content, conversion,
sales enablement, data collection, measurement, and marketing
technology. The website is a connective layer within the marketing
ecosystem. It is not the entire marketing ecosystem. Strategy and client
requirements should guide implementation. Themes, CMS platforms,
templates, layouts, technologies, and features should not determine
strategy.

    THEME_LAYOUT != STRATEGY

## 18. Commercial Governance

Pricing, scope, discounts, commercial exceptions, proposal terms, and
offer economics must retrieve from approved current commercial sources.
Historical proposals, competitor pricing, old price points, and previous
custom arrangements may provide context. They do not establish current
SimpliCreative pricing.

    CURRENT_PRICING_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH_OVERRIDES_HISTORICAL_COMMERCIAL_EXAMPLES

If required commercial authority is unavailable:

    MISSING_AUTHORITY_REQUIRES_HUMAN_INPUT

## 22. Automation

Approved automation candidates may include: Transcript-to-decision
capture. Meeting preparation. ScoreApp-to-Diagnostic Brief. Acquisition
intelligence preparation. Approved Diagnostic-to-proposal drafting.
Blueprint research synthesis. Strategy Deck drafting. Templated
WordPress questionnaire and content-outline preparation. CARE reporting
preparation. Discoverability opportunity analysis. Project knowledge
capture. CRM enrichment. Source classification. Low-risk internal
reminders. Automation should preserve source links, data quality,
validation state, owner, approval state, client boundary, and decision
trace. Automate only after the underlying human process is understood
and validated. Material client, strategic, commercial, financial,
contractual, publishing, campaign-spend, or production actions require
explicit human authorization.

context a senior SimpliCreative team member would need.

  **Context Type**           **Purpose**

  Identity Context           Who SimpliCreative is

## 30. Conflict Resolution

When two current governing sources materially disagree:

    AGENCY_SOURCE_CONFLICT_REQUIRES_HUMAN_REVIEW

AI should identify the conflicting sources, conflicting assertions,
affected task, potential consequence, and required human decision. AI
should not resolve the conflict by choosing the newest file
automatically, choosing the most frequently repeated language, choosing
the strongest semantic match, averaging the positions, or increasing its
confidence. When governing information is missing:

    MISSING_AUTHORITY_REQUIRES_HUMAN_INPUT

AI should not substitute general model knowledge, competitor practice,
an external methodology, a client example, or historical information for
missing SimpliCreative policy.

## 31. Gold-Standard Corpus

Gold Standards calibrate reasoning quality, depth, structure, judgment,
presentation, and application. They do not establish policy. Every
Gold-Standard or reference example should preserve: Why it is useful.
Inputs used. Frameworks used. Important decisions. Reusable reasoning
patterns. Context boundaries. Known gaps. Assumptions. Approval state.
Source artifact. Examples containing invented or assumed evidence remain
reference examples until validated through real work. Example copy
should carry:

    Knowledge_Type = REFERENCE_EXAMPLE
    Authority = NON_GOVERNING
    Copy_Authority = FALSE
    Reuse = PRINCIPLE_ONLY

## 32. Internal Reasoning Library

The Second Brain should preserve the intelligence behind finished
deliverables, including: Assessment responses. Questionnaire data. Sales
Intelligence. Evidence Records. Diagnostic Records. Hypothesis records.
Root-cause analysis. Prioritization records. Recommendation Records.
Strategist decisions. Rejected alternatives. Implementation
dependencies. Client feedback. Campaign tests. Performance findings.
Outcome records. The objective is to preserve how conclusions were
reached rather than only how the final deliverable looked.

## 33. Historical Library

Historical materials should remain available for provenance without
participating in normal current-state reasoning. This includes previous
audits, proposals, roadmaps, strategy versions, old campaign strategies,
previous pricing, old ICP definitions, retired frameworks, superseded
SOPs, prior Second Brain versions, and historical client work.
Historical sources should carry:

    Current_Decision_Authority = FALSE
    Normal_Retrieval = FALSE

When v1.2 is approved:

    v1.0 = SUPERSEDED
    v1.1 = SUPERSEDED
    v1.2 = CURRENT_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH

## 34. Security & Client Isolation

Client-specific information must remain bounded to the appropriate
client context. The system should not reuse one client's confidential
strategy, deliverable language, performance data, transcript content,
pricing arrangement, stakeholder information, or proprietary evidence
for another client. Access controls should account for user role, client
boundary, source classification, sensitivity, and workflow requirements.
If a task requires an unauthorized client or data-boundary crossing:

    STOP → REQUIRE HUMAN RESOLUTION

## 40. Final Engineering Principle

The Second Brain should know more than what it retrieved. It should know
what kind of source it found, whether that source is current, what it is
allowed to influence, how strong the underlying evidence is, whose
context it belongs to, what restrictions travel with it, what decisions
have already been made, and where human judgment is still required. The
system should make SimpliCreative's intelligence easier to access
without making SimpliCreative's judgment invisible. The final operating
rule is:

    AI CAN RETRIEVE THE KNOWLEDGE, CONNECT THE EVIDENCE, DRAFT THE WORK, AND FIND THE PATTERN.
    HUMANS REMAIN ACCOUNTABLE FOR WHAT SIMPLICREATIVE BELIEVES, RECOMMENDS, COMMITS TO, PUBLISHES, SPENDS, AND LEARNS.
