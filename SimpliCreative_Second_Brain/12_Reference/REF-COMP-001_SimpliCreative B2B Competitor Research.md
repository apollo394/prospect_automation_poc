# **B2B Strategic Website Consultancy Competitive Intelligence and Pricing Architecture Analysis**

## **Competitive Landscape and Structural Categorization**

The market for B2B digital engagements is heavily divided between production-oriented web development agencies that execute tactical assets and strategic growth consultancies that treat the digital foundation as an engine for enterprise pipeline generation. For a specialized consultancy such as SimpliCreative—which positions the corporate website as the connective tissue linking positioning, narrative messaging, search and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO/GEO), CRM architecture, conversion paths, and analytics—benchmarking against generic digital agencies produces invalid pricing assumptions.  
To establish an accurate strategic baseline, market participants are classified into four distinct operational cohorts based on buyer alignment, strategic depth, technical delivery models, and commercial structures.

### **Direct Competitors**

Direct competitors operate with a business model closely aligned with SimpliCreative, serving mid-market B2B organizations (typically 20 to 500 employees) with lean marketing departments. These firms integrate strategic discovery, positioning, UX/UI, content architecture, Webflow or WordPress CMS engineering, analytics infrastructure, and ongoing growth retainers under a single operating model.

* **Gorilla 76**: Specializes in mid-sized B2B industrial and manufacturing firms with $10M to $200M in revenue1. The firm enforces a mandatory, paid upfront diagnostic strategy phase before any build or ongoing retainer is contracted, creating the closest structural match to SimpliCreative's delivery ladder2.  
* **Blend (Blend B2B)**: Focuses on mid-market, private equity-backed B2B technology and services companies3. Blend explicitly frames the corporate website and HubSpot CRM as the central revenue engine, providing published starting prices across decoupled strategy roadmaps, bespoke website builds, and integrated retainer tiers, including commercialized Answer Engine Optimization3.  
* **Orbit Media Studios**: A Chicago-based digital firm targeting mid-market marketing directors and CMOs5. The company combines consultative discovery with custom B2B lead generation websites, conversion rate optimization (CRO), and search strategy5.  
* **Bop Design**: Dedicated exclusively to B2B website design, brand strategy, and content marketing for mid-market firms7. Bop Design positions the website as the central hub of B2B marketing, emphasizing audience-centric information architecture, buyer journeys, and conversion paths7.  
* **Huemor**: Focuses on mid-market B2B SaaS and commercial organizations9. Huemor integrates customer research, brand messaging, custom UX/UI, and full-stack development, utilizing structured pricing calculators to manage scope expectations9.  
* **Altitude Marketing**: Serves B2B companies in complex, highly technical, and regulated sectors such as life sciences, manufacturing, and enterprise software12. The firm operates an integrated retainer model covering positioning, web development, SEO, and demand generation for lean teams13.  
* **SmartBug Media**: An Elite HubSpot partner operating across mid-market B2B organizations15. SmartBug delivers full revenue growth engagements combining digital strategy, custom website design, and complex CRM/RevOps integrations15.  
* **Ironpaper**: Focuses strictly on B2B pipeline generation, lead acquisition, and sales enablement16. The agency positions the website as an active conversion and nurturing mechanism tied directly to CRM infrastructure and business outcomes16.  
* **Vital Design**: Operates across B2B technology and manufacturing, offering full-funnel web strategy, brand messaging, custom web development, and digital marketing retainers18.  
* **Brolik**: A growth consultancy combining business consulting, digital strategy, brand positioning, and custom web builds for growth-stage businesses20.  
* **Flightpath**: A digital agency serving mid-market to enterprise clients, focusing on the intersection of digital foundation, UX, web development, and campaign execution22.

### **Aspirational Peers**

Aspirational peers share a similar strategic philosophy but target larger mid-market to enterprise clients ($30M to $1B+ revenue), commanding substantially higher budgets, larger delivery pods, and extended multi-quarter transformation roadmaps.

* **Webstacks**: Operates at the enterprise and high-growth SaaS tier, building composable, headless web architectures (Next.js, Gatsby, headless CMS) integrated with sophisticated data layers23. The firm serves clients with dedicated digital teams and large capital allocations24.  
* **Refine Labs**: Operates a demand creation and revenue transformation model for mid-market and enterprise B2B SaaS firms at $30M to $50M+ ARR25. Refine Labs structures engagements around high-ticket diagnostics followed by multi-quarter demand and revenue transformation retainers25.  
* **Directive Consulting**: Known for its proprietary "Customer Generation" methodology, Directive targets mid-market and enterprise B2B SaaS ($10M+ ARR)28. The firm treats SEO, Answer Engine Optimization (GEO/AEO), paid media, and CRO as an interconnected pipeline generation system tied directly to customer acquisition cost (CAC) and customer lifetime value (LTV)28.  
* **Walker Sands**: A full-service B2B consultancy integrating public relations, brand strategy, demand generation, and enterprise web development for major technology and professional service firms30.

### **Adjacent Alternatives**

Adjacent alternatives compete for overlapping marketing budgets but specialize in a single functional discipline, such as executive leadership, organic search, or positioning.

* **Kalungi**: A fractional CMO and outsourced marketing department model built specifically for B2B SaaS32. Rather than positioning around the website build, Kalungi provides fractional executive leadership and execution across all go-to-market functions32.  
* **SimpleTiger**: A specialized SaaS search agency offering SEO, GEO/AEO, and high-velocity conversion landing page design and build sprints under subscription and tiered retainer models33.  
* **SERPsculpt**: A boutique consultancy offering bottom-of-funnel search strategy, GEO frameworks, and content execution for founder-led SaaS26.

### **Excluded Models (Not Comparable)**

* **WebFX**: Excluded from direct pricing conclusions due to its high-volume, automated assembly-line operational structure35. WebFX relies on commoditized, template-driven delivery and entry-level maintenance packages that lack executive-level strategic integration35.

## **Market Pattern Analysis**

The operational models across direct competitors and aspirational peers reflect defined mechanics governing entry-point packaging, scope pricing, ongoing retainers, and value differentiation.

### **Entry-Point Architecture and Strategy Decoupling**

The strategic B2B digital sector has structurally evolved away from rolling discovery into a monolithic implementation contract. Across the competitive set, approximately 60% of high-performing consultancies formally decouple the strategic diagnostic phase from downstream website execution2.  
Firms that separate strategy do so via branded, standalone products with fixed pricing. Gorilla 76 packages its "Road Map" at a fixed $12,000, framing it as a mandatory diagnostic and prescription phase before any build or ongoing retainer is contracted2. Blend packages a "Demand Generation Strategy" starting at $13,0004. Refine Labs commands $35,000 for its 6-week "Revenue Performance Assessment"26, while Directive prices technical SEO audits and attribution blueprints between $10,000 and $30,00029.  
The primary mechanism that makes this decoupled model effective is platform independence. Rather than positioning the discovery as a preliminary phase of a pre-determined website build, consultancies deliver an implementation-ready strategic asset that the client owns outright2. By stating that the client can execute the strategy internally, with existing vendors, or through the consultancy, the agency eliminates the conflict of interest inherent in traditional agency pitching2. Strategy fees are almost never credited 100% toward subsequent implementation; instead, the strategy stands on its own economic merit, filtering out low-budget buyers while establishing high advisory authority2.

To handle scope uncertainty when exact site requirements are unknown prior to diagnostic discovery, leading agencies utilize two distinct pricing frameworks. This establishes clear financial expectations before detailed architectural scoping occurs during the strategy engagement4. Second, agencies such as Orbit Media and Huemor utilize modular pricing menus and online cost calculators that categorize complexity by page-count bands (10–50 pages vs. 50–150 pages) and specific technical features (e.g., custom animation, CRM integration depth, copywriting scope)5.

### **Ongoing Retainer Packaging and Value Models**

The B2B consultancy market has largely moved away from commoditized "hourly bucket" retainers, which penalize operational efficiency and commoditize agency capabilities39. Modern retainers are packaged into four primary structures:

> 1. **Capability-Tiered Programs**: Blend structures ongoing demand generation retainers starting at $8,000/month and Answer Engine Optimization at $7,500/month (Essential) to $14,000/month (Advanced)4. Directive packages enterprise search into Tier 1 ($7,000–$15,000/month), Tier 2 ($15,000–$30,000/month), and Tier 3 ($30,000–$60,000+/month), with deliverables scaled by market competitiveness29.  
> 2. **Full-Service Pod / Outsourced Department**: Altitude Marketing packages integrated B2B retainers at $9,500–$12,500/month, providing cross-functional execution across branding, web development, SEO, and paid campaigns for lean marketing teams13. Refine Labs structures full-service demand generation at $26,000–$31,000/month with 6-month minimum commitments25.  
> 3. **Advisory and Strategic Guidance**: Gorilla 76 provides a strategic coaching retainer at $4,000–$5,000/month for clients executing the roadmap internally, scaling to $12,500–$25,000/month ($150,000–$300,000/year) for full execution1. Kalungi offers fractional CMO advisory at $2,000–$5,000/month, scaling to $15,000–$40,000+/month for an embedded CMO plus full execution team32.  
> 4. **Technical Foundation & Iterative Optimization**: Huemor provides structured post-launch optimization tiers from $500–$1,000/month (mid-sized) to $5,000–$15,000/month (enterprise) covering conversion optimization, heatmaps, and GA4 maintenance10. Orbit Media provides ongoing website enhancements through dedicated Web Strategists billing at $175/hour in predefined SOW blocks38.

## **Strategic Pricing Architecture Recommendations**

The following structural pricing recommendations are synthesized from empirical competitor behavior, offering specific architectural models for SimpliCreative to evaluate.

### **Recommendation 1: Establish SimpliBlueprint as a Fixed-Fee Standalone Strategic Asset**

* **Market Evidence**: Gorilla 76 standardizes its upfront diagnostic "Road Map" at a fixed $12,000 fee across all client sizes2. Blend packages its "Demand Generation Strategy" starting at $13,0004, while Refine Labs charges $35,000 for a 6-week diagnostic26. These engagements are sold as independent, platform-agnostic deliverables with no contractual requirement for downstream build commitments2.  
* **Interpretation**: Mid-market B2B buyers with lean teams resist committing to $75,000+ implementation contracts with new vendors before alignment is established. A fixed-price, standalone strategic engagement lowers the sales barrier, shortens the sales cycle, and de-risks the engagement for the client while demonstrating strategic authority2.  
* **Actionable Recommendation**: Structure the **SimpliBlueprint** as a standardized fixed-fee package in the **$12,500 to $16,500** range. Explicitly position the deliverable as a complete, platform-agnostic strategic blueprint that the client owns fully and can implement internally or with external partners. Avoid offering a 100% fee credit against the subsequent build, as this devalues the strategy; instead, test a time-sensitive closing credit (e.g., $2,500) applied toward the SimpliFoundation contract if signed within 30 days of Blueprint delivery.

### **Recommendation 2: Structure Implementation Tiers Around Architectural Complexity**

* **Market Evidence**: Blend and Huemor anchor implementation pricing by publishing explicit starting tiers ($45,000 bespoke and $100,000 enterprise at Blend4; $25,000–$75,000 mid-sized and $100,000–$250,000 enterprise at Huemor10). Both firms explicitly connect these pricing tiers to page volume, CMS data structure, and CRM integration requirements4.  
* **Interpretation**: Providing fully custom proposals without transparent pricing anchors increases sales friction and invites scope disputes during negotiation. Pre-establishing distinct complexity tiers filters out unqualified leads early while framing the value of higher-complexity builds4.  
* **Actionable Recommendation**: Establish three standardized scope brackets for **SimpliFoundation**, with the final fixed scope determined during the SimpliBlueprint engagement:  
  * *Core Foundation Tier* (15–30 pages, custom Webflow/WordPress CMS, brand messaging framework, standard HubSpot/Salesforce forms, GA4 event tracking): **$45,000 – $60,000**.  
  * *Growth Foundation Tier* (30–60 pages, interactive component library, advanced RevOps routing, dynamic personalization, full AEO/GEO schema architecture): **$65,000 – $85,000**.  
  * *Enterprise Foundation Tier* (60+ pages, multi-region localization, complex third-party API/ERP middleware, comprehensive design system): **$95,000 – $140,000+**.

### **Recommendation 3: Commercialize Answer Engine Optimization (AEO/GEO) as a Premium Capability**

* **Market Evidence**: Blend offers dedicated Answer Engine Optimization retainers starting at $7,500/month (Essential) and $14,000/month (Advanced)4. Directive incorporates proprietary AI search optimization (Stratos AI) into enterprise retainers commanding $15,000 to $60,000+/month28.  
* **Interpretation**: Traditional keyword SEO is widely perceived as commoditized, driving down retainer price realization39. In contrast, B2B executives are actively seeking solutions for generative search visibility across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Overviews4. Packaging AEO/GEO capabilities creates significant pricing power4.  
* **Actionable Recommendation**: Productize AEO/GEO capabilities across all stages of the SimpliCreative ladder. Within the SimpliBlueprint, include a formal AI recommendation visibility audit. Within SimpliFoundation, build AI-ready structured data, entity architectures, and authoritative citations. Within SimpliCARE, offer continuous prompt-tracking and citation defense sprints.

### **Recommendation 4: Structure SimpliCARE as a Dual-Track Retainer Model**

* **Market Evidence**: Gorilla 76 separates low-touch strategic coaching ($4,000–$5,000/month) from full-scale program implementation ($12,500–$25,000/month)1. Altitude Marketing prices integrated team execution at $9,500–$12,500/month14, while technical maintenance retainers range from $500 to $1,500/month for mid-market platforms10.  
* **Interpretation**: A single retainer structure fails to accommodate the differing operational capacities of lean marketing teams. Some clients have internal execution resources and only require executive strategic steering, while others need hands-on technical and content execution2.  
* **Actionable Recommendation**: Structure **SimpliCARE** around two distinct commercial tracks:  
  * *Strategic Steering & Foundation Assurance Track* (Monthly executive strategy sessions, quarterly business reviews, AEO visibility monitoring, core Webflow/WordPress maintenance, GA4 analytics audits): **$3,500 – $5,000 / month**.  
  * *Active Growth & Optimization Pod Track* (All steering elements plus ongoing CRO split testing, monthly AEO content sprints, landing page development, and CRM workflow optimization): **$8,500 – $13,500 / month**.
