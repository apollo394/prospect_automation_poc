## 10. CARE Delivery Operating Rules

Routine requests may move directly through normal delivery.

Strategic changes involving audience, positioning, messaging, offers, buyer journey, major CTA logic, information architecture, content priorities, or success definition require strategist review.

Large redesigns, major integrations, extensive custom development, major strategic work, and substantial new responsibility do not silently become CARE.

CARE prioritization is not purely first-in, first-out.

Evidence-led optimization uses:

`OBSERVATION → HYPOTHESIS → CHANGE → MEASUREMENT → LEARNING`

## 12. Engineering Acceptance Standard

The system passes this fixture when it can assemble the activation record from approved commercial, strategic, Foundation, website, analytics, CRM, partner, and client sources without inventing missing responsibility.

If required activation information is missing, the system should preserve the gap and request human resolution.

It must not activate CARE solely because a proposal exists.

It must not treat a signed proposal as equivalent to delivery activation unless the approved commercial activation conditions are satisfied.

It must preserve client / partner responsibility boundaries.

It must preserve known measurement limitations.

It must create the first-priority state without converting every open item into urgent work.
