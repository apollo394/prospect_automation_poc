## 13. Engineering Acceptance Standard

The system passes this fixture when it can close a CARE engagement without losing material context.

It must preserve the difference between unfinished work and cancelled work.

It must not remove access automatically without an approved action.

It must not store passwords or secret credential values in the exit record.

It must preserve final measurement definitions, limitations, and approved outcome history.

It must route durable client knowledge through the approved memory process.

It must not convert a client-specific result into agency methodology.

It must preserve ownership after SimpliCreative is no longer responsible.

It must not imply that commercial close, delivery close, access removal, and knowledge retention are the same event.
