## Purpose

This artifact demonstrates the approved structured-intelligence layer that sits between questionnaire validation / research and client-facing Blueprint generation.

It is not the final Blueprint Report. It preserves the evidence, validation status, reasoning, uncertainty, findings, hypotheses, priorities, recommendations, and implementation implications that the Report and Strategy Deck must trace back to.
