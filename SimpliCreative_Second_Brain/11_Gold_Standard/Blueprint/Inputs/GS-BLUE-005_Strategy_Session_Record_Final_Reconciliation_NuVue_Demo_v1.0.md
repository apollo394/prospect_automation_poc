## Purpose

This artifact demonstrates the required post-Strategy Session record and final intelligence reconciliation that occurs before Blueprint approval and implementation handoff.

It preserves client feedback without automatically converting that feedback into strategic truth. It records what changed, what remained unchanged, which decisions became final, which evidence boundaries survive, and what the approved implementation handoff may inherit.
