# Engineer Handoff Manifest

Version: 1.3 Classification: Engineering Handoff Status: Final Owner:
SimpliCreative Primary Users: AI Engineers, System Architects, Technical
Implementation Partners Purpose: Master index and orientation guide for
the SimpliCreative AI Second Brain engineering package

## 1. Purpose

This Manifest is the starting point for implementation of the
SimpliCreative AI Second Brain. It identifies: What documents are
included. What each document governs. Which documents have authority.
Which materials are supporting or reference-only. Which documents should
be read first. Which sources must not govern current system behavior.
Which decisions are intentionally reserved for engineering discovery.
This Manifest does not replace any governing strategy, SOP, Source
Register entry, or Engineering Specification. Its job is to tell the
engineering team how the complete package fits together.

## 2. What We Are Building

SimpliCreative is building a governed AI Intelligence Layer. The
objective is not to create a chatbot with access to a large document
library. The system should understand:

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Requirement**      **Meaning**
  -------------------- --------------------------------------------------
  Source Authority     Which information is allowed to govern

  Version              What is current versus superseded

  Context              What information is relevant to the task

  Evidence             What is known versus inferred

  Client Boundary      Which information belongs to which client

  Decision History     What was decided and why

  Human Authority      What requires human judgment

  Learning             What may become durable agency knowledge

  Source Restrictions  How reference and external material may be used
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

The governing principle is:

    RELEVANCE DETERMINES WHAT TO RETRIEVE
    AUTHORITY DETERMINES WHAT MAY GOVERN

## 17. Historical Corpus

The Historical collection should contain previous strategy, prior
positioning, old ICP definitions, previous pricing, retired offers, old

Historical information should only enter context when history,
evolution, or provenance is relevant to the task.

## 22. Conflict Handling

When current governing sources materially conflict:

    AGENCY_SOURCE_CONFLICT_REQUIRES_HUMAN_REVIEW

The system should surface: Conflicting sources. Conflicting assertions.
Affected task. Potential consequence. Required human decision. The AI
should not resolve the conflict based solely on recency, embedding
similarity, repetition, or model confidence.

## 23. Missing Authority

When the required governing information does not exist:

    MISSING_AUTHORITY_REQUIRES_HUMAN_INPUT

The system may explain what is missing. It must not silently substitute
generic model knowledge, competitor practices, external methodology,
historical information, or client examples for missing SimpliCreative
policy.

## 26. Human Approval Boundaries

Engineering should treat approval as a workflow state. Human approval is
required for material strategic conclusions, Diagnostic fit and routing,
Blueprint conclusions, final recommendations, pricing, scope, commercial
exceptions, contractual commitments, partner commitments, material
external publication, final paid creative, campaign activation, media
spend, consequential production changes, final client deliverables,
legal or compliance-sensitive language, Agency Semantic Memory
promotion, and changes to governing agency strategy.

## 29. Acceptance Testing

Engineering acceptance tests should be provided separately in the
`15_Evaluations` collection. At minimum, the system must demonstrate
that it can: Select current strategy over superseded strategy. Select
current pricing over historical pricing. Keep Client A information out
of Client B context. Prevent an Assessment result from becoming a
root-cause diagnosis. Keep Ashley methodology subordinate to
SimpliCreative strategy. Recognize examples as non-governing. Preserve
source restrictions through generation. Prevent client deliverables from
becoming reusable copy. Preserve uncertainty when evidence is
incomplete. Require human approval where defined. Prevent campaign
results from automatically becoming agency knowledge. Surface
governing-source conflicts. Surface missing authority rather than
inventing policy.

## 32. Final Orientation for Engineering

The three documents at the center of the implementation have different
jobs.

  **Document**              **Question It Answers**
  ------------------------- --------------------------------------
  Master Architecture       How must the Second Brain behave?
  Source Register           What knowledge is allowed to govern?
  Engineering Build Brief   What needs to be built?

The Engineer Handoff Manifest tells the implementation team how those
pieces and the supporting corpus fit together. The objective is not
simply to give AI more SimpliCreative information. The objective is to
give it the right information, under the right authority, for the right
client and task, with the right evidence boundaries and human controls.

    AI FINDS THE PATTERN. HUMANS DECIDE WHAT IT MEANS.
