# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind frontend; FastAPI + Pydantic backend — specified in the POC brief.

## Users

Primary user is Lei Lani Fera (and SimpliCreative strategists) during prospect qualification after a strategy call. They currently stitch Fathom transcripts through multiple AI tools and manual analysis to produce questionnaires, assessments, and service recommendations. Job: turn call evidence into reviewed strategic deliverables quickly, without losing human judgment.

## Product Purpose

**Prospect Intelligence** is an internal SimpliCreative POC that streamlines Prospect → Call Analysis → Strategic Assessment → Scope → Pricing → Proposal. Success means a client demo can show time saved versus the multi-tool manual path, with every insight tied to transcript evidence and human approval before anything is treated as final.

## Positioning

AI prepares the work; humans review and approve. Insights are evidence-linked to strategy-call transcripts. FAST readiness is a visible website-delivery lens with four reviewed pillars. It has no overall score and does not automate strategist judgment.

## Operating Context

Demo centerpiece is Apex Metrics. Flow: Prospects dashboard → Apex Metrics → Analyze Prospect → Call Intelligence + Evidence → Questionnaire → Strategic Assessment + FAST Readiness → Recommended Services → Scope → Pricing → Proposal → Human approval. Work queue surfaces next actions. Knowledge/Frameworks shows methodology status as pending configuration.

## Capabilities and Constraints

In scope: prospect list, work queue, analyze animation, intelligence categories, evidence, questionnaire accept/edit/reject, assessment with human review, evidence-backed FAST readiness, recommended services, static demo rate card, scope / pricing / proposal drafts with approve-edit-regenerate, mock AI provider, FastAPI demo data.

Out of scope: Agency Brain branding, Monday/Slack/CRM/portal/PM, GA/GSC/SparkToro, payments, e-sign/PDF export, production auth/RBAC, live pricing APIs, FAST scoring, website crawls, and live analytics integrations.

Undecided: live AI provider for commercial drafts (currently mock/seed), real Apex Metrics transcript, client-supplied methodology criteria.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence over claims — every material insight cites a transcript source.
2. Human in the loop — AI drafts; strategists approve.
3. Configurable methodology — frameworks are structured but empty of proprietary rules until the client provides them.
4. Attention-first workspace — dashboard shows what needs action, not vanity KPIs.
5. SimpliCreative identity — the tool should feel like their studio, not generic SaaS.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for body text and controls; keyboard-reachable primary actions; visible focus states.
